# Deep-pass ledger template

This is a tracked blank template. Do not record an actual case here.
Copy it to `_private/deep-pass/ledger.md` in the instance, or to the protected location declared by the
instance's rules. If a ledger already exists, append to that ledger instead of replacing it with this file.
Confirm that the destination is ignored and is not already tracked. Instance backup is separate.

The procedure is `system/deep-pass.md`, Recording and follow-up. Keep one record per pass, even when the
judgment is unchanged. An outside-pass decision can be recorded when a lens materially changes it.
A count of records or used lenses is not a measure of effectiveness.

## Record fields

- **ID and date:** a stable local ID and the observation date.
- **Question and evidence boundary:** the question, evidence cutoff, sources actually read, and unavailable material.
- **Candidates:** named lenses and task-specific perspectives considered; include the most consequential omission when relevant.
- **Used perspectives:** those actually applied, with the question or test they contributed.
- **Before and after:** original judgment, attack or check, resulting judgment, and why it changed or survived.
- **Correction source:** source lookup, worked example, fresh reader, model review, owner, or execution. Record the actual model when used; distinguish same-family review from a different family. Unavailable review is not completed review.
- **Follow-up:** the observation that could support or reverse the decision, its local review time or trigger, and the observed result when available.

Add dated corrections and follow-up outcomes to the original ID. Preserve the original observation instead
of silently rewriting it. Keep private and company-derived material within the instance boundary; an
anonymous-looking summary is not automatically publishable.
