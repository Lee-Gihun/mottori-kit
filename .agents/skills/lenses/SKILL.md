# Thinking lenses

Thirteen questions. Each one looks at an answer from a side that is easy to miss. They are for a person
first: when you are stuck, find the row of the table that describes your situation and ask its
question. No agent is needed.

An agent uses them the same way. Work the problem your own way first. Then look at the table once:
before you commit to a design, a decision or something that cannot be undone, or when the same fix has
failed twice. Whether any question on it is worth asking is your call: ask one when it could change
what you are about to commit to, and skip the table for routine or quick questions.

1. Find the one or two rows whose "When" fits and whose question could break your answer. If none
   fits, use the open question below the table, or none.
2. Answer the question properly. Its file has the method, an example and the ways to misuse it.
3. Show the owner the question. End with one line that names the lens, puts its question in the
   terms of this case, and says what it changed in your answer or that the answer held, for example:
   "Lens (fence): what was the old pipeline preventing, and is that still needed? It changed the plan:
   switch it off today, delete it after month-end." The owner thinks with a question they can see. A
   check they cannot see helps only you. The line stays when the answer held: it is one of the clues
   rule 5 of `AGENTS.md` keeps.

Two limits:

- In text the owner will send or sign (an announcement, a message, a plan with their name on it), add
  no promise, date or condition the owner did not state. If a lens says one is needed, put it beside
  the draft as a suggestion with its reason in a sentence, and let the owner decide.
- Keep your reply the size the owner's request needs. A warning that would change what the owner does
  stays, however short the reply is.

|---|---|---|
| You see only the gains and not the costs, or you hear "both get better" | What is scarce here and must be shared, and who pays for the gain? | [ledger.md](ledger.md) |
| You can explain it with terms but have never worked it out yourself | Do you know only the name, or how it works? Did you calculate a small example? | [feynman.md](feynman.md) |
| You will run or send it soon, and only reasons for success have piled up | What would make it fail for certain? What is the cheapest way to check whether you are wrong? | [inversion.md](inversion.md) |
| You must decide a count, a size or a range | What happens if you push the value to zero and to infinity? At what value does the behaviour change? | [limits.md](limits.md) |
| You are stuck and looking only inside your own field, or want to know whether an idea is new | Has another field already met what makes this problem hard? | [isomorphism.md](isomorphism.md) |
| You want to remove a practice whose reason you do not know, or to replace it with something new | What was the existing practice preventing? Does that condition still exist? | [fence.md](fence.md) |
| You must split limited time or money across several places, or are unsure whether to go on | Where would the next hour improve things the most? | [marginal.md](marginal.md) |
| You decide where to stand in a company, a product or a career | Is this the time to choose which field to be in, or to do well in the one already chosen? | [jensen.md](jensen.md) |
| A judgment about yourself, or an agent's judgment about its own work, is about to be used as evidence | Does anything on record support this judgment, or only my own impression? | [mirror.md](mirror.md) |
| The speaker gains from the conclusion, or you are preparing a request or a proposal | Which conclusion benefits this person? Would they have said it even if it were not true? | [incentive.md](incentive.md) |
| A failure would end the chance to try again, or someone says "once is fine" | If it goes wrong, is there another chance? What happens if you repeat it a hundred times? | [ergodic.md](ergodic.md) |
| You are looking only at successes, at people who answered, at what you found; or you are about to write "there is none" | What could never have entered this data in the first place? | [silence.md](silence.md) |
| People or systems adapt to your action, or things return to how they were after you fix them | If you act, how does the other side respond, and after how long? | [feedback-loop.md](feedback-loop.md) |

The table is not the whole view. When no row fits, or the rows you used agreed with you too easily,
ask one more question: if this table missed one thing about this case, who would see it at once, and
what would they check first? Pick someone who has lived this exact problem, by role and not by name,
and borrow how they work, not how they talk. A question from outside the table counts the same as a
listed one when it gives you an objection or an answer you did not already have. Show it to the owner
the same way, as "Lens (outside the table)".

Do not go through every row. Do not credit a lens you did not use.
